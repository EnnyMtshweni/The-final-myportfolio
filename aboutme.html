    My Story

    I didn't get interested in fintech because it was trendy—I stayed because it's one of the few industries where technology, data, and trust intersect every day. Every transaction, dashboard, and decision has a real impact on people and businesses, which is what makes the work meaningful to me.

    I'm Enny Mtshweni, an aspiring software engineer and data analyst with a growing interest in building reliable, data-driven financial products. My focus is on backend development, business analytics, and creating systems that are dependable, secure, and easy to understand. I enjoy solving problems that require both technical thinking and attention to detail, whether that's developing applications, analyzing datasets, or improving business processes through data.

    MAs I continue building my technical skills in JavaScript, Python, SQL, Power BI, and modern web technologies, I'm especially drawn to fintech because it demands more than code—it demands accuracy, accountability, and resilience. I'm fascinated by how financial systems handle transactions, protect user data, and remain reliable under pressure, and I enjoy learning how these systems are designed.

    Outside of projects, I spend much of my time strengthening my technical skills, studying real-world fintech products, exploring backend architecture, and learning from engineering case studies and postmortems. I'm equally interested in understanding the business side of technology, using analytics to uncover insights that help organizations make better decisions. My goal is to build technology that people can trust—whether that's through reliable software, meaningful data insights, or products that solve real financial challenges.
