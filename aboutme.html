My Story

I didn’t become interested in technology simply because coding was something I could learn. I became interested because I realised that technology can be used to understand problems, improve the way people work, and create solutions that make a real difference. That is what continues to drive my interest in both data and software development..

I’m Enny Mtshweni, an aspiring Data and Full-Stack Developer who enjoys working at the intersection of technology, data, and business. I’m interested in understanding the problem behind the code, not just building something that works. Whether I’m developing an application, working with data, building dashboards, or integrating an API, I want to understand how the solution creates value and solves a real need..

My growing experience in full-stack development and data analytics has taught me to approach problems from different perspectives. I enjoy working with databases, building applications, analysing data, and turning information into something that can support better decisions. I’m particularly interested in areas such as fintech, consulting, and other industries where technology can improve efficiency, solve business challenges, and create better experiences for people.

Beyond my technical skills, I bring curiosity, accountability, resilience, and a willingness to learn. I’m comfortable asking questions, researching when I don’t understand something, taking feedback, and working with others to move a project forward. I’ve also learned that being a good developer is not only about technical ability, it is about communication, collaboration, prioritisation, attention to detail, and taking responsibility for the work you produce..

As I continue growing, my goal is to become a strong full-stack developer with a deeper understanding of data, databases, APIs, cloud technologies, and business problem-solving. I want to work on meaningful projects where I can keep learning, contribute from day one, and build technology that is reliable, useful, and genuinely solves problems.
