FutureMeBank

An AI-powered financial literacy ecosystem for children aged 7–17, designed to build money confidence before banking becomes the focus.

Overview

Designing financial confidence before banking.

FutureMeBank is a fintech concept designed to help children build healthy financial habits before they become traditional banking customers.

Rather than focusing only on account ownership and saving, the product teaches children how to think about money, understand value, plan for goals and make better financial choices as they grow.

7–17 Age group

MVP Roadmap

Problem

Most youth products teach saving — not understanding.

Many children’s banking products focus on opening an account and encouraging basic saving. I saw a different opportunity: helping young users understand the principles of money before they become traditional customers.

  • Children need financial concepts explained in engaging and age-appropriate ways.
  • Parents need visibility into their child’s progress.
  • Financial literacy needs to be interactive rather than static.
  • Schools and banks could become future engagement partners.

Solution

A financial literacy ecosystem, not just a bank app.

I designed FutureMeBank as an AI-powered financial education platform that combines lessons, goals, rewards, knowledge checks and guidance into one product experience.

The core idea was to teach financial behaviour first, with banking integration becoming a future addition rather than the starting point.

Child dashboard

A central place for learning, progress, rewards and personal goals.

Parent dashboard

Parents can track learning progress and support financial habits.

Financial lessons

Short, practical lessons on saving, budgeting, value and goals.

Savings goals

Helps children understand delayed gratification and planning.

AI coach

Provides personalised guidance based on progress and needs.

Investment simulator

Introduces investing safely in a learning environment.

Research & Product Thinking

Designing for multiple users and real-life contexts.

The biggest challenge was understanding that this was not a single-user product. Children, parents, schools and banks all interact with the ecosystem differently.

I researched South African banking products, Chinese family banking models, fintech trends and financial literacy to define a more meaningful product direction grounded in user behaviour and market thinking.

Technical Direction

Scalable architecture for a growing ecosystem.

The planned product architecture included:

  • React frontend
  • Python / FastAPI backend
  • PostgreSQL database
  • REST APIs
  • Authentication
  • AI integration
  • Future IoT and banking opportunities

What I Learned

Product thinking starts before the code.

Research matters

Strong products grow from real user understanding rather than assumptions.

Problem-first mindset

I learned to focus on why a product matters, not only what can be built.

Build for the future

A clear MVP and roadmap help turn a concept into something scalable.

Final Reflection

Don’t build what you can code — build what should exist.

FutureMeBank taught me that good product design begins with understanding the user, the problem and the context around the solution.
